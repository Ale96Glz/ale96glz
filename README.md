## About

Computer Science Engineer with **8+ years** of full-stack development at the **CEIGE** and **CIGE** centers of **UCI**. I built and maintained institutional systems: the Customs portal and internal management (**VUA** and **GINA**), the digital policy registry for **SISEN**, and **Fiscalix** components for the Attorney General's Office. Scope ran from requirements to critical modules (document intake, policy registration, justice workflows) with Java, Spring, PHP, Symfony, Oracle and PostgreSQL, under strict business rules.

Since **2025** I have moved into software quality audits and test automation. At **SIVEI**, **UAM**, I have been a functional auditor, automation engineer and QA line lead: API suites with **Karate** against Swagger, load and stress testing with **Gatling**, and UI/data cross-checks against synthetic voting datasets at national-election scale (Python, Excel). In the open, a microservices e-commerce system and its test suite. I am looking for **remote** part-time or contract work. I work in **Spanish** and **English** (**C1**, EF SET).
